# Credit Card Terms in Brief

## Interest and repayments

Interest is charged on any balance carried past the payment due date shown on the statement. Paying the statement balance in full by the due date means purchases stay interest free for the full interest free period. Paying only the minimum amount keeps the account in good standing but interest applies to the remaining balance from the transaction date.

## Card blocking and replacement

A card reported lost or stolen is blocked immediately and cannot be unblocked. A replacement card with a new number is dispatched to the registered address within five working days. Recurring payments set up on the old card number must be moved to the new card by the customer.

## Disputed transactions

A transaction can be disputed within 60 days of the statement date on which it appears. While a dispute is open the disputed amount does not accrue interest. If the dispute is resolved in the customer's favour the amount and any related fees are reversed.

## Fees

Fees are listed in the fee schedule current at the time the fee is applied. The bank gives 30 days notice before a fee change takes effect.
